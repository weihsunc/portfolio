Goal

Illoca started with Tracing Paper, an AI drafting tool for architects. When Plamo pulled stronger traction with EPC and BIM teams, the company pivoted in that direction. My job: 0 to 1 design of the end to end flow, so users can create high quality, adjustable models and export them into their own workflow.

  • Product: From Tracing Paper to Plamo: sign up, onboarding, first model, the workspace and monetization.

The pivot

After Tracing Paper launched, people were signing up, but conversion was weak. While the team was demoing our research preview of the vibe modeling workflow, we saw a clear need, especially from the GC, EPC, developer and owner side, for a usable model to collaborate on, instead of drafting in 2D first and turning it into 3D.

Problems

  • Trust: AI 3D usually means a dead mesh. These users need geometry they can keep editing.
  • Learning curve: They know Revit and Rhino, not prompting.
  • Agent vs. controls: What belongs to the agent, what to the panels, and does the agent know what you changed by hand?
  • Value before paywall: Credits are abstract. Users need to feel the value before they upgrade.

Design directions

The end to end design takes users from getting started, through their first model and its parameters, to quick access to iterations and control panels.
